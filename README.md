Privacy Policy for لهج (Lahaj)

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Thank you for using لهج ("Lahaj", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

لهج is designed with privacy at its core as an offline-first Islamic utility:

No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in with credentials to use the application.

No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or IP address.

Local On-Device Storage: All user data (including Quran reading bookmarks, saved Athkar favorites, tasbeeh counters, font size preferences, and display modes) is stored locally and exclusively within your device's sandbox environment. We have no access to this data.

2. Permissions and Device Features

Notifications (Optional): If you choose to enable daily Athkar or prayer reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device. No device push tokens or notification preferences are transmitted to external servers.

Network Access: Any internet access requested by the app is used solely for downloading or updating in-app assets (such as Quran text pages or optional audio files). No usage profiling or telemetry data is sent across the network.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

No Third-Party Analytics: We do not include third-party tracking software, behavioral analytics tools, or user profiling SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Tracking).

No Advertising Networks: The app does not integrate commercial ad networks and does not track users using the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

App Store Diagnostics: Apple may gather aggregated, anonymized crash logs and installation metrics if you have opted in via iOS system settings. Such data is governed under Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under applicable age limits in your jurisdiction). It is fully safe for users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly within the app or on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
